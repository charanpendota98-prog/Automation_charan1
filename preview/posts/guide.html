How to check an opportunity before applying

Use the official notification as the final source before you submit an application. Check the eligibility, dates, fee, selection process and official application link in one sitting.

A simple checklist

Confirm the recruiting organisation, read the eligibility section, note the closing date and save the official PDF or webpage. Never send money, OTPs, passwords or identity documents through an unverified contact.

If a date, fee or deadline is not stated in the official notification, treat it as not announced rather than guessing.
