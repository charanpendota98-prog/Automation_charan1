Engineering students: internship updates 2026

Use this guide to check whether an internship opportunity is genuine and useful before sharing documents or accepting an offer.

Check before you apply

Verify the organisation, work location, role, duration, stipend statement and official contact. If a fee, date or stipend is not announced in the official source, do not assume it.

Do not pay an upfront registration fee or share OTPs, passwords or bank credentials for an internship application.
