Aptitude · SSC / Banking Moderate
1 A train 240 m long crosses a telegraph pole in 12 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?

Why: Speed = Distance / Time = 240 m / 12 s = 20 m/s. Converting to km/h: 20 × (18 / 5) = 72 km/h. · Quantitative Aptitude (SSC & Banking)

Reasoning · SSC CGL Easy
2 In a certain code language, if 'PAPER' is written as 'QBQFS', how is 'OFFICE' written in that code?

Why: Each letter is shifted forward by +1: O(+1)=P, F(+1)=G, F(+1)=G, I(+1)=J, C(+1)=D, E(+1)=F. · Logical Reasoning (SSC CGL & RRB)

Banking · RBI Policy Moderate
3 Which key policy rate does the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) adjust to inject short-term liquidity into commercial banks?

Why: Repo Rate is the interest rate at which RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks against government securities to infuse liquidity. · rbi.org.in

Polity · Supreme Court Easy
4 Under which Article of the Constitution can a citizen approach the Supreme Court directly for the enforcement of Fundamental Rights?

Why: Article 32 gives the right to move the Supreme Court by appropriate proceedings for enforcement of Fundamental Rights (empowering it to issue writs). · Constitution of India

Economy · GST Moderate
5 Which Constitutional Amendment Act introduced the nationwide Goods and Services Tax (GST) in India?

Why: The 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016 introduced the unified Goods and Services Tax (GST) nationwide with effect from 1 July 2017. · Ministry of Finance

TSPSC Group 1/2 · History Advanced
6 Who was the founder of the Asaf Jahi dynasty in Hyderabad Deccan?

Why: Nizam-ul-Mulk founded the Asaf Jahi dynasty in 1724 after defeating Mubariz Khan at the Battle of Shakar Kheda. · TSPSC Syllabus / State History

TSPSC · Polity Moderate
7 Under which Article of the Indian Constitution was the state of Telangana formed in 2014?

Why: Article 3 empowers Parliament to form new States and alter boundaries or names of existing States by law. · Constitution of India

TSPSC · Culture Advanced
8 The iconic Telangana Martyrs Memorial at Gun Park, Hyderabad was sculpted by which renowned artist?

Why: Prof. Aekka Yadagiri Rao sculpted the historic Gun Park Martyrs Memorial in memory of the 1969 agitation martyrs. · Telangana Sahitya Akademi

TSPSC · Projects Moderate
9 Which is the first barrage of the Kaleshwaram Lift Irrigation Project located at the Godavari-Pranahita confluence?

Why: Medigadda (Lakshmi) Barrage is the headworks where water is lifted from the Pranahita-Godavari confluence. · Irrigation & CAD Dept

APPSC Group 1/2 · Geography Moderate
10 The Polavaram Multi-Purpose National Irrigation Project is being constructed across which river in Andhra Pradesh?

Why: Polavaram Project is built across the Godavari River in Eluru and Alluri Sitharama Raju districts of AP. · AP Water Resources Dept

APPSC · Reorganisation Advanced
11 Under which section of the AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 does the Governor have special responsibilities for Hyderabad as common capital?

Why: Section 8 of the AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 vests special responsibility in the Governor for law and order in the common capital. · AP Reorganisation Gazette

AP DSC · Education Moderate
12 According to the RTE Act, 2009, what is the pupil-teacher ratio for primary schools (classes 1 to 5)?

Why: The RTE Act specifies a Pupil-Teacher Ratio (PTR) of 30:1 for primary schools up to 200 enrolled students. · Ministry of Education

SSC CGL · Mathematics Advanced
13 A sum of money invested at compound interest amounts to Rs. 4,840 in 2 years and Rs. 5,324 in 3 years. Find the rate of interest.

Why: Interest for 3rd year = Rs. 5324 - 4840 = Rs. 484. Rate = (484 / 4840) * 100 = 10% per annum. · SSC CGL Tier 1

SSC · History Moderate
14 Which Indus Valley Civilization site features the world's earliest known tidal dockyard?

Why: Lothal in Gujarat had a tidal dockyard connected to the ancient Sabarmati river channels for sea trade. · Archaeological Survey of India

Banking · DICGC Moderate
15 What is the maximum deposit insurance coverage provided by DICGC per depositor per insured bank in India?

Why: DICGC insures savings, fixed, current and recurring deposits up to Rs. 5 Lakhs (principal + interest). · DICGC / RBI

Banking · Union Budget Moderate
16 In the Union Budget of India, how is the 'Primary Deficit' calculated?

Why: Primary Deficit = Fiscal Deficit - Interest Payments. It indicates government borrowing requirements excluding past interest obligations. · Ministry of Finance

RRB NTPC · Railways GK Moderate
17 Which is the longest running passenger train route by both distance and time in Indian Railways?

Why: Vivek Express covers 4,189 km from Dibrugarh in Assam to Kanyakumari over approximately 75 hours. · Indian Railways / RRB

RRB · Space Tech Advanced
18 What propellants are utilized in the indigenous CE-20 Cryogenic Upper Stage of ISRO's LVM3 rocket?

Why: CE-20 operates burning cryogenic Liquid Hydrogen (fuel at -253 deg C) and Liquid Oxygen (oxidizer at -183 deg C). · ISRO Technical Reports

RRB Group D · Physics Easy
19 What is the SI unit of electrical resistance in the International System of Units?

Why: The SI unit of electrical resistance is the Ohm (Omega), named after German physicist Georg Simon Ohm. · NCERT Class 10 Science

General Science · Biology Easy
20 What is the chemical name of Vitamin C, an essential water-soluble antioxidant vitamin?

Why: Vitamin C is chemically known as Ascorbic Acid; deficiency causes scurvy characterized by bleeding gums and fatigue. · NCERT Biology
