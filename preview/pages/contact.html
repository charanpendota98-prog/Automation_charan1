Students Internet Center — apply without travelling

You do not need to visit any centre, cyber café or office to apply for a job or scholarship. Call us and WhatsApp your documents — we complete the application and send you the PDF. Service charge is kept as low as possible.

  3. We apply & send the PDF — the filled application PDF reaches you on WhatsApp; corrections free.
