Editorial policy

Every post is published only after clearing these 5 gates

Every post has to clear these 5 gates before publishing. If a gate fails, the post stops — we do not let it through saying "good enough".

① Source check (fact guard)Every date and number must match the source document — if it does not match, it is auto-flagged.
② Deep cross-verification2+ sources, official ones preferred; conflicting dates or stale-year data block publishing. Confidence score 0–100.
③ Originality, minimum 72%Never a copy of other sites; a close match (≥62% similarity) is not published.
④ Human reviewThe draft comes first; publishing happens only after the quality score crosses 80/100.
⑤ Advertising policySPONSORED label is compulsory; no ads beside links; clickbait and fake clicks are completely banned.

Source policy

  • Sources: government portals (.gov.in), official notifications, reliable news agencies — social media rumours are never accepted as a source.
  • At least two sources for a topic; if there is no official source, the post stops.
  • Citations appear inside every article; there is no agency copy.

Correction policy

  • If a mistake is found, we usually correct it within 24 hours and add a correction note.
  • To request a correction: studentupinformative@gmail.com — send the link, the mistake and the correct official source.
  • If the mistake is serious, the article is paused temporarily and republished only after verification.

Advertising and editorial independence

Advertisements never influence editorial decisions. Sponsored content always appears separately with a SPONSORED label. Ad links carry rel="sponsored nofollow". After AdSense approval, the number of ads follows policy limits.
