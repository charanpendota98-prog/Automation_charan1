Privacy policy

Your data is yours — what we collect and what we never collect

This policy covers the studentup.in website, the exam portal and the daily poll. In one line: we do not collect your name, phone number or Aadhaar details.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts or login — we do not ask for name, email or phone number.
  • No documents, bank details or OTP — none of it.
  • Personal data is never sold or rented to anyone.

Ads and measurement

  • Google AdSense / Analytics may be used for ads and measurement — they can use cookies. They start only after the owner's approval.
  • Until AdSense approval, the ads shown are our own partners' creatives, clearly marked with a SPONSORED label.
  • What we give advertisers is display space only — never your personal data.

Information you email us

Any email you send is used only to make the correction. Once the correction is done there is no need to keep it.

Your rights

If you believe we hold anything about you, ask us to delete it — email us. If this policy changes, the updated date on this page changes with it.
